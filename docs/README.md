# Documentation index

- [Start here](start_here.md): initialize a new project; removed after setup.
- [Research program](research_program.md): scientific intent and evaluation.
- [Current state](state.md): handoff and active runs.
- [Infrastructure](infrastructure.md): environment, compute, budget, and recovery.
- [Template design](template_design.md): adaptation notes; removed after setup.
- `research/`: source-grounded literature notes and open proposals.
- `findings/`: retained evidence and conclusions, including negative results.
- `decisions/`: consequential choices and their rationale.
- `writeups/`: reader-facing drafts linked to canonical findings.

Add links to substantive records as they are created. `_template.md` files are
prompts to copy, not research records. Experiment READMEs describe executable
work; findings describe what its evidence supports.
